HousePulse shows how electricity flows through your home, live, on your Homey dashboard.

Add the Energy Flow widget and see at a glance how much power comes from the grid and your solar panels, and where it goes: your home, your EV charger and your home battery. Animated lines show the direction of each flow, and get thicker as the power increases.

Features
- Live power for grid, solar, EV charger and home battery, with home consumption in the centre
- State of charge for your home battery and your electric car, shown as a ring around the node
- Today's totals: solar production, energy bought and sold, and EV charging (kWh)
- Self-sufficiency, right now and for today
- Works with most brands: devices are recognised through Homey's own energy settings
- Hybrid inverters and dongles that report solar, grid and battery in one device are supported: pick the exact value you want
- Sets itself up: without configuration the widget suggests your energy devices automatically
- Up to two solar inverters, two EV chargers and two home batteries
- Marks values that have not been updated for a while, and devices that are offline
- Hints when a sign setting is likely wrong
- Light and dark mode, adapts its height to the dashboard

Getting started
Add the Energy Flow widget to a dashboard. It picks your devices automatically. Edit the widget to choose other devices, or select "None" to remove one.
